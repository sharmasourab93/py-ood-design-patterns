# Creational Design Patterns

Creational patterns focus on object creation. They help when direct construction becomes too rigid, too repetitive, or too tightly coupled to concrete classes.

## Patterns In This Section

- [Abstract Factory](abstract_factory/README.md)
- [Builder](builder/README.md)
- [Factory](factory/README.md)
- [Prototype](prototype/README.md)
- [Singleton](singleton/README.md)

## What To Look For

- who creates the object
- how much the caller knows about concrete classes
- whether construction steps should be hidden or reused
